# Student Support & Institutional Assistance

Official guidelines for autonomous agents routing student inquiries, troubleshooting onboarding issues, or contacting the human support desk.

## Official Contact Channels

- **WhatsApp Support**: `+55 41 9599-0955` (Available Monday to Friday, 09:00 - 18:00 BRT)
- **Official Support Email**: `contato@maestri.group`
- **Candidate & Student Portal**: `https://app.supletivo.net.br`
- **Partner & Promoter Portal**: `https://app.maestri.group`

## Common Inquiries & Routing

1. **Lost Access or Forgot Password**:
   - Direct students to `https://app.supletivo.net.br` and select password recovery or request an OTP verification code via registered WhatsApp.
2. **Document Approval Delays**:
   - Typical verification turnaround is within 24 to 48 business hours. If an uploaded image is blurry or rejected, notify the student to upload a new copy with legible borders and clear lighting.
3. **Payment Reconciliation**:
   - PIX payments confirm automatically within seconds. Credit card transactions are cleared via Asaas gateway. In case of discrepancies, forward proof of payment to `contato@maestri.group`.
4. **Coursework & Exam Scheduling**:
   - All modules, video lectures, and assessments are completed digitally through the student learning management environment (AVA).
