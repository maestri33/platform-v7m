# Maestri Group Educational Promoter Program

Comprehensive guide for autonomous agents operating with or assisting educational promoters, regional coordinators, and affiliate partners of the Maestri Group ecosystem.

## Overview

The Maestri Group Promoter Program enables partners to earn commissions by referring students to accredited online adult education (EJA Supletivo) and technical programs.

## Key Promoter Features

- **Unique Referral Links**: Each promoter receives personalized referral tracking URLs (`https://supletivo.net.br/?ref=PROMOTER_CODE` and `https://app.supletivo.net.br/matricula?ref=PROMOTER_CODE`).
- **Instant Commission Tracking**: Real-time attribution of leads, payments, and commission balances via the Unified Portal.
- **Automated PIX Payouts**: Request commission withdrawals directly to a registered PIX key (CPF, phone, or CNPJ).
- **Marketing Creatives**: Access to approved promotional banners, video creatives, flyers, and presentation decks.

## Onboarding as a Promoter

1. **Review Opportunity**: Visit `https://maestri.group/apresentacao` for the official compensation overview and commercial deck.
2. **Access Promoter Hub**: Log in at `https://app.maestri.group` (or `https://hub.maestri.group` for regional leaders).
3. **Obtain Referral Badge & Link**: Copy your unique link to share with prospects.
4. **Lead Nurturing**: Follow lead statuses through the dashboard:
   - `lead`: Candidate initiated contact or checkout.
   - `paid`: Payment approved; commission credited to promoter balance.
   - `enrolled`: Student submitted required documentation and started classes.
