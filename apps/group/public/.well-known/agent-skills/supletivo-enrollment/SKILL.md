# Supletivo EJA Online Enrollment

Comprehensive guide for autonomous agents assisting students with accelerated adult education (EJA — Educação de Jovens e Adultos) enrollment for Elementary (Ensino Fundamental) and High School (Ensino Médio) diplomas.

## Eligibility Requirements

In accordance with Brazilian Federal Education Law (Lei nº 9.394/1996 — LDB):
- **Ensino Fundamental (6º ao 9º ano)**: Minimum age of **15 years old**.
- **Ensino Médio (1º ao 3º ano)**: Minimum age of **18 years old**.

## Required Documentation

Candidates must present clear digital copies or photographs of the following documents during online enrollment:
1. **Official Photo ID**: RG (Registro Geral) or CNH (Carteira Nacional de Habilitação).
2. **CPF**: Cadastro de Pessoas Físicas (if not already included in RG/CNH).
3. **Proof of Residence**: Utility bill (water, electricity, gas, or internet) issued within the last 90 days.
4. **Academic History / Transcripts**: Prior school transcripts (Histórico Escolar) or statement of conclusion of prior grades, if available.
5. **Biometric Face Photo (Selfie)**: Clear front-facing selfie taken in good lighting for identity verification.

## Enrollment Steps

1. **Access the Enrollment Portal**: Navigate to `https://app.supletivo.net.br/matricula`.
2. **Identify Candidate**: Provide full name, valid CPF, mobile phone with WhatsApp, and email address.
3. **Select Program**:
   - Ensino Fundamental
   - Ensino Médio
   - Combo (Fundamental + Médio)
4. **Document Upload**: Upload clear photos of RG/CNH, CPF, proof of residence, and take a verification selfie.
5. **Tuition Checkout**: Payment is available via instant PIX (with dynamic QR code and copy-paste key) or credit card installment.
6. **Confirmation & Onboarding**: Once payment is cleared, student credentials and access to the virtual learning environment (AVA) are dispatched automatically via WhatsApp and email.
